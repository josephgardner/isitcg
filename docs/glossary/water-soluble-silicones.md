# Water Soluble Silicones

**Verdict:** CG Approved

These are exceptions that are OK: (The raw ingredients dissolve in water and rinse off with plain water. Shampoo may or may not be necessary to remove products containing these ingredients).

## Common ingredients

- Dimethicone Copolyol
- Dimethicone Hydroxypropyl Trimonium Chloride
- Dimethicone-PG Diethylmonium Chloride
- Glycidoxy Dimethicone Crosspolymer
- Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein Hydroxypropyl Polysiloxane
- Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein Hydroxypropyl Polysiloxane (water soluble)
- Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein PG-Propyl Silanetriol
- Hydrolyzed Wheat protein/hydroxypropyl polysiloxane and cystine/silicone co-polymers
