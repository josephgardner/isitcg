# Itchy M's

**Verdict:** CG Approved with caution

These ingredients are preservatives from the family of substances called Isothiozalinones and are added to products to prevent the growth of micro-organisms. These ingredients should be avoided if you have sensitive skin, as they can make your scalp itchy and irritated
