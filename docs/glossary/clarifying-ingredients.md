# Clarifying Ingredients

**Verdict:** CG Approved with caution

Shampoos with clarifying ingredients remove product and oil build up. These ingredients are not as harsh as sulfates, but should be used sparingly (avg of two times/month)

> This page is a stub. [Edit it on GitHub](https://github.com/josephgardner/isitcg/edit/main/docs/glossary/clarifying-ingredients.md) to add more detail.

## Common ingredients

- C14-16 Olefin sulfonate
- Cajuput
- Citronella grass
- Cyclodextrin
- Cymbopogon
- Cymbopogon citratus
- CYMBOPOGON FLEXUOSUS (LEMONGRASS) LEAF OIL
- CYMBOPOGON SCHOENANTHUS (LEMONGRASS) EXTRACT
